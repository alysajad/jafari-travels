Tour Overview

Experience the magic of 'Paradise on Earth' with our curated 6-day Kashmir odyssey. From the tranquil waters of Dal Lake in Srinagar to the snow-capped meadows of Gulmarg and the breathtaking landscapes of Pahalgam, this journey offers the perfect blend of adventure and serenity.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Srinagar airport. Meet our representative and transfer to a luxury houseboat. Enjoy a romantic sunset Shikara ride on Dal Lake, visiting the floating gardens and vegetable market.
